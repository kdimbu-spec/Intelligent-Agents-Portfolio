### Week 4 Hybrid Agent Architectures

#This week focused on comparing deliberative, reactive, and hybrid agent architectures. The key objective was to understand how different architectures support intelligent behaviour and to evaluate their suitability for real-world applications. The seminar examined the Subsumption Architecture, Touring Machine, InteRRaP, and 3T Architecture, supported by an Autonomous Mobile Robot (AMR) warehouse case study. 

## Evidence of Learning
The 3T Architecture was identified as a practical hybrid approach that combines reactive behaviour with long-term planning. It consists of a Reactive Tier, Sequencer Tier, and Deliberative Tier. 
## Critical Analysis
A key insight from this week's study was that no single agent architecture is universally superior. Deliberative agents provide strong planning capabilities but may respond too slowly in dynamic environments. Reactive agents respond quickly but lack strategic reasoning. Hybrid architectures address these limitations by combining both approaches, making them particularly suitable for autonomous systems operating in uncertain environments. However, they introduce greater design complexity and require effective coordination between layers.
