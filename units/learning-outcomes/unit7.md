# Unit 7: Natural Language Processing (NLP)

## Overview
This unit explored **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**, a key branch of Artificial Intelligence that enables machines to understand, interpret, and generate human language. The module examined why human language is challenging for computers to process and introduced the core concepts, techniques, and technologies used in modern NLP systems.

## Learning Objectives
During this unit, I:

- Explored the concepts and principles that underpin NLP.
- Examined different approaches used in NLP applications.
- Investigated technologies and tools that support NLP development.
- Evaluated the challenges involved in designing and deploying NLP systems.
- Developed an understanding of the core principles behind language-processing technologies.

## Key Topics Covered
### Introduction to NLP
- Definition and importance of NLP in Artificial Intelligence.
- Human language complexity and computational challenges.
- Applications of NLP in intelligent systems and agents.

### NLP Techniques
- Text preprocessing and tokenization.
- Semantic and syntactic analysis.
- Language modelling techniques.
- Machine learning approaches to language understanding.

### Word Embeddings and Word2Vec
- Understanding word representations in vector space.
- Learning semantic relationships between words.
- Applying Word2Vec for contextual language analysis.

## Challenges in NLP
The unit highlighted several difficulties encountered when developing NLP systems, including:

- Language ambiguity.
- Context interpretation.
- Variations in grammar and syntax.
- Multilingual language processing.
- Data quality and bias considerations.

## Practical Applications
Examples of NLP applications discussed include:

- Chatbots and virtual assistants.
- Machine translation systems.
- Sentiment analysis.
- Information retrieval systems.
- Intelligent conversational agents.

## Skills Developed
Through this unit, I gained experience in:

- Understanding fundamental NLP concepts and methodologies.
- Evaluating different NLP approaches and technologies.
- Analysing the strengths and limitations of NLP systems.
- Applying word embedding concepts such as Word2Vec.
- Assessing real-world NLP applications within intelligent agents and AI solutions.

## Reflection
This unit provided a strong foundation in Natural Language Processing and its role in Artificial Intelligence. Understanding how machines process and interpret human language highlighted both the opportunities and challenges of developing intelligent systems that can communicate effectively with users. The exploration of Word2Vec and modern NLP techniques demonstrated how advances in language modelling continue to improve the capabilities of intelligent agents and conversational AI systems.
